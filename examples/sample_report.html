HighC04 Risk managementmissing3ECC 1-5, NIS2 (a)No formal risk registerAdopt a method (e.g. ISO 27005 style), keep a risk register with owners and treatment plans, and reassess at least yearly or after major changes.
HighC11 Least privilege and access reviewsmissing3ECC 2-2, NIS2 (i)Several staff have standing domain admin rightsRemove standing admin rights, separate admin accounts, and run quarterly access reviews for critical systems.
HighC19 Vulnerability managementmissing3ECC 2-10, NIS2 (e)No regular vulnerability scanningScan at least monthly, prioritise by CVSS, exploitability and asset criticality, and track fixes against SLAs.
HighC22 Incident responsemissing3ECC 2-13, NIS2 (b)No incident response planWrite an IR plan with roles and playbooks, run a tabletop exercise yearly, and document regulator reporting duties (NIS2: early warning within 24h, notification within 72h).
HighC25 Business continuitymissing3ECC 3-1, NIS2 (c)BCP does not cover cyber scenariosAdd cyber scenarios to BCP/DR, define recovery time objectives and test the plan yearly.
HighC06 Compliance and auditmissing2ECC 1-7, ECC 1-8, NIS2 (f)No internal audit of security controlsPlan a yearly control review, track findings to closure and report results to management.
HighC16 Data classification and protectionmissing2ECC 2-7, NIS2 (a)No data classification schemeDefine a classification scheme, label key data stores and apply handling rules (access, sharing, DLP) per class.
HighC20 Penetration testingmissing2ECC 2-11, NIS2 (f)Never pen testedCommission a pen test of external and critical systems at least yearly and after major changes; retest fixes.
HighC26 Third-party securitymissing2ECC 4-1, NIS2 (d)No supplier security assessmentsTier suppliers by risk, send security questionnaires to critical ones and add security and breach-notification clauses to contracts.
MediumC09 Asset inventorypartial1.5ECC 2-1, NIS2 (i)Hardware listed; software and owners missingBuild an asset inventory (CMDB or spreadsheet to start), assign owners and classify assets by criticality.
MediumC10 Multi-factor authenticationpartial1.5ECC 2-2, NIS2 (j)MFA on email only; VPN and admin accounts not coveredEnforce MFA starting with admins, VPN and email; prefer phishing-resistant methods (FIDO2/authenticator apps over SMS).
MediumC18 Backup and recoverypartial1.5ECC 2-9, NIS2 (c)Nightly backups but no offline copy and no restore testFollow 3-2-1 backups with one offline/immutable copy and test restores at least quarterly.
MediumC21 Logging and monitoringpartial1.5ECC 2-12, NIS2 (b)Logs collected but nobody reviews alertsForward logs from critical systems to a SIEM, define retention, and build alert use cases with someone responsible for triage.
MediumC01 Cybersecurity strategypartial1ECC 1-1, NIS2 (a)Strategy drafted but not yet approved by the boardWrite a 2-3 year strategy aligned to business goals, get it signed off by leadership and review it yearly.
MediumC03 Security policiespartial1ECC 1-3, NIS2 (a), NIS2 (f)Policies exist but last reviewed in 2023Create a policy set (acceptable use, access control, incident response, etc.), get approval, publish it and review at least yearly.
MediumC05 Security in IT projectspartial1ECC 1-6, NIS2 (e)Security review only for large projectsAdd security checkpoints to the project lifecycle: requirements, design review, code scanning and a pre-release security test.
MediumC08 Awareness and trainingpartial1ECC 1-10, NIS2 (g)Yearly training only; no phishing simulationsRun onboarding and yearly training, plus quarterly phishing simulations; track completion rates.
MediumC13 Email protectionpartial1ECC 2-4, NIS2 (g)SPF and DKIM set; DMARC at p=noneEnable advanced email filtering and publish SPF, DKIM and a DMARC policy, moving towards p=reject.
MediumC14 Network securitypartial1ECC 2-5, NIS2 (e)Flat office network; servers in separate VLANSegment networks by trust level, default-deny between zones and review firewall rules every 6 months.
MediumC24 Web application securitypartial1ECC 2-15, NIS2 (e)Public site behind CDN; no WAF; never tested against OWASP Top 10Put a WAF in front of public apps, enforce HTTPS and security headers, and test against the OWASP Top 10 before release.
MediumC27 Cloud securitypartial1ECC 4-2, NIS2 (d)Data hosted in-region; CIS benchmark review not doneReview cloud configs against CIS benchmarks, enable cloud logging and confirm data residency rules are met.
